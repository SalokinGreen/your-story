# Which models fit an AI game master — research, 2026-10-21

A research pass, not a decision. It recommends models for each job the game
gives a model, and says where the evidence is thin. Gathered by two Haiku
research agents and cross-checked against primary sources where it mattered
(OpenRouter's live model list, Anthropic's usage policy). Prices and
leaderboards move fast; re-check anything here older than a month.

## The bottom line

- **No benchmark tests a model as a game master.** The closest are
  creative-writing boards (prose quality), tool-use boards (agentic
  reliability) and a few roleplay and D&D studies. Every recommendation
  below combines evidence from separate tests; none comes from one that
  tests the whole job.
- **The evidence points to Claude for prose**, with GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3 as
  the strongest open-weight models.
- **For tool use, the leaders are Claude, Kimi K3 and Qwen 3.8**, on thin and
  partly self-reported aggregator data.
- **The cheap tier has improved a lot.** Claude Haiku 5.5, GLM-5.3 Flash and
  DeepSeek V4.1 Flash all cost $0.10–0.15 in and $0.50–0.60 out per million
  tokens.
- **Content tolerance is barely measured.** It matters for an 18+
  dark-fantasy game, and it is the biggest unknown (§4).
- **The default tiers look dated.** Mistral Nemo, Mistral Small 3.2, Grok 4.5
  and Kimi K3 are the defaults, and the catalog has stale entries (§6).
- **The real answer is a bake-off on our own prompts** (§7).

## 1. What each job needs

| job in the game | what matters | what matters less |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy GM tiers (combat and rules; boss fights and campaign-shaping turns) | correct tool calls across ~56 tools and up to ~10 rounds, following the protocol (dice report, `calculate` judges), long-context recall, reasoning | speed |
| Light GM tiers (banter, ordinary checks) | speed, cost, staying in voice, valid tool calls on simple turns | deep reasoning |
| Narration (the story stage, streamed to the player) | **prose quality**, voice, pacing, no "AI-isms", tolerance for dark content | tool use (none) |
| Side agents (five Observer judges, Memory Keeper, Director, Reflection, compaction) | cheap, fast, valid JSON, short answers | prose |
| The Game Designer | tool use plus creative judgement | speed |

  1. Claude Fable 5.1
  2. Claude Opus 5 (xhigh)
  3. **Claude Opus 5.5**
  4. GPT-6 Astra

  6. **GLM-5.3**
  7. Claude Fable 5
  8. GPT-5.6 Sol
  9. GPT-5.5
  10. **Kimi K3**

  Sonnet 5.5, Gemini, Grok and DeepSeek are outside the top 10.
- **EQ-Bench Creative Writing v3** (secondhand, data from 2026-09-01): Claude
  Opus 5, Kimi K3 and GLM-5.3 are the top three. **The judge is a Claude
  model, and the page says it doesn't control for self-preference**
  (https://eqbench.com/about.html). Its "Slop" column is the closest measure
  of AI-isms.
- **Arena creative writing:** Claude Opus 5.5 is provisionally #1 (2026-09-26,
  wide rank range). Gemini 3 Pro was #5 in August.
- **TRACE Bench** (a roleplay checklist; arXiv 2608.11236, July 2026): Gemma-4-31B,
  GPT-5.5, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Kimi K2.5 and DeepSeek V4 Pro are at the top,
  all within 2 points. It doesn't separate the frontier models much.
- **What GM products use** (vendor claims): Friends & Fables runs GLM 4.7 as a
  premium narration model (December 2025). NovelAI's newer model is reportedly
  GLM-based. GLM recurs as the open-weight choice for long-form roleplay.
- **D&D studies:** a UCSD study (January 2026) found Claude 3.5 Haiku the most
  reliable of the models tested in tool-grounded D&D. These are older models.
  RPGBench (2025) found LLMs tell engaging stories but struggle to keep game
  mechanics consistent. That struggle is exactly the gap this app's engine
  covers.

## 4. Content tolerance: the biggest unknown for an 18+ game

- **Claude:** Anthropic's usage policy (effective 2026-11-12, quoted from
  https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) prohibits content that "Promote[s],
  trivialize[s], or glorify[ies] graphic violence, gore, sexual violence",
  and all sexually explicit content ("Depict or request sexual intercourse or
  sex acts", "Engage in erotic chats"). It has **no general carve-out for
  fiction or roleplay**. Ordinary dark-fantasy violence and horror is not
  "glorifying", but no test measures how often Claude softens or refuses it.
  Erotic content is off the table on Claude, on every route, the
  subscription included.
- **Grok** is the most permissive, and has the worst documented safety
  record (Common Sense Media, January 2026). That's a brand risk, not just a
  feature.
- **Gemini:** API safety filters are off by default from 2.5 on. Users report
  creative-writing responses erased mid-generation (anecdotal).
- **GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek, Mistral:** no 2026 measurement. A December 2025
  mirror of the UGI board showed older Mistral Large models as very willing,
  and older Kimi K2 models as reluctant. One community report says GLM-5.3
  "talks scenes toward safer ground".

## 5. Recommendations by job

These are starting points to test, not settled answers.

| job | on the desktop, with your Claude subscription | anywhere, pay per token | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy GM tier (combat and rules) | **Claude Sonnet (subscription)** | **GLM 5.3 (Z.ai)**, or Claude Sonnet 5.5 | Sonnet: strong agentic results, prose near Opus, $0 marginal cost. GLM-5.3: top-6 prose, a third of Sonnet's price, and your token packages |
| Top GM tier (boss fights, campaign calls) | **Claude Opus (subscription)** | **Claude Opus 5.5**, or Kimi K3 | Opus 5.5 leads both prose and tool use. Kimi K3 is strong but slow (40 tok/s) |
| Light GM tiers | **Claude Haiku (subscription)** | **Claude Haiku 5.5**, or GLM 5.3 Flash | Haiku 5.5 is the fastest (235 tok/s) and among the cheapest. GLM Flash adds forced-thinking latency |
| Narration | **Claude Sonnet (subscription)** | **GLM 5.3 (Z.ai)**, or Claude Sonnet 5.5 | Prose quality matters most here. Opus is overkill for every story pass |
| Side agents | keep them off the subscription (decision 12) | **Claude Haiku 5.5**, or GLM 5.3 Flash or DeepSeek V4.1 Flash | Cheap, fast, short JSON. Mistral Small 3.2 remains a fine fallback |
| Game Designer | **Claude Sonnet (subscription)** | Claude Sonnet 5.5 or GLM 5.3 | Tool use plus creative judgement |

**Caveats that change the picture:**
- If the game should allow **explicit sexual content**, Claude can't be the
  narrator for those scenes on any route (§4). That points to GLM, Kimi or
  Mistral for narration.
- On the subscription route, each GM round costs about 3 s of CLI launch,
  and the first-round tool miss is being fixed now.

## 7. What would settle it: a GM bake-off on our own prompts

No public test measures this job, so the evidence that would decide it is our
own. This is the status report's "Frontier 5 — eval harness", which has
never been built. A small version:

1. **Tool reliability:** about 20 recorded GM turns replayed through 4–5
   shortlisted models, counting invalid JSON, wrong tools, tool calls leaked
   as text, protocol breaks (judging a roll inside the roll tool), and
   provider 400s.
2. **Recall:** facts planted in real campaign notes at 32k, 64k and 128k.
3. **Side-agent validity:** about 50 real Observer and Memory Keeper prompts,
   checked for schema-valid JSON.
4. **Prose and tone:** the same 10 scenes narrated by each candidate, judged
   blind by the author. Not judged by a model, given the self-preference
   problem in §2.

Every one of these spends real money on the author's keys (or the
subscription), so it needs the author's go and a budget.

# 2026-10-24 — Blood & Bone, expanded: character options (E3)

Plan: [`bb-expansion-design.md`](../bb-expansion-design.md) §7b. Decisions
150–159. Branch `claude/bb-characters`, off master, beside E2 (#238).

Seven third Archetypes, one per Kind, each with two Archetype Powers and two
more Kind Powers, in its own note because the Kind notes sit near the read
cap: the Courtier (the approved sample, word for word), the Pilgrim, the
Shepherd, the Hound, the Apostate, the Penitent and the Seeker. And thirty
new assets across five notes. About 9,300 tokens, half the roadmap's guess:
the writers kept each Archetype tight rather than pad it.

The review was about balance, against rules written into the brief: a
relic that gave an automatic weak hit per knot (nine of them, now once a
session), a nail that pinned a creature forever (now a scene), a Wendigo
power at Stress -3 with no roll (now -2, matching the Kind's strongest), a
Hunter power with no cost (now Stress +1), a Raven at +2 where every asset
gives +1. And two names used twice, a Path and an Archetype both called the
Pilgrim, a Power and a Rite both called the Tithe.

The live-block token test pinned a fixed ceiling that every expansion step
broke; it now compares the compact list against one line per note.

Nothing on screen changed. The reading copy is the artifact in the PR.

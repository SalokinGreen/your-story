# 2026-10-24 — Blood & Bone, expanded: four new Kinds, and Powers that always give an edge (E4)

Plan: [`bb-expansion-design.md`](../bb-expansion-design.md) §7c. Decisions
160 and 161. Branch `claude/bb-kinds`, off master.

Asked for two or three new Kinds, the author picked all four offered: the
Revenant (back in its own body for one purpose), the Changeling (bargains
and iron), the Doppelgänger (stolen faces, stolen lives) and the Rusalka
(the drowned of rivers and lakes). Each is a full Kind note in the shape of
the seven, with two Archetypes, a Beast track and ten Powers, and a
creation-prompts table, plus its own entries in the People and Feeding
tables. No new meter sets. The list of Kinds now lives in one place, so the
next Kind is one line there and a test that fails until every per-Kind table
has it.

Mid-build the author set a rule: "all tiers of a power should give a
mechanical advantage because we don't want to gatekeep cool stuff behind
xp". The fiction already lets a Gargoyle listen to stone; the Power should
make it better at it. The new Kinds were written to it, and 86 of the
original seven Kinds' Power abilities were given an edge (the rest already
had one and are unchanged word for word). E3's Archetype Powers got the
same pass on their own branch (#239).

Balance fixes in review: two automatic strong hits that repeated every scene
(now once a session), a bargain upgrade with no cost to the Changeling (now
Stress +1, and it binds them too), and a weak-to-strong upgrade on a first
ability (now an add on a different move).

No screenshots: nothing on screen changed.

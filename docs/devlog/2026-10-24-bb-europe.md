# 2026-10-24 — Blood & Bone, expanded: Europe by Night, deeper (E2)

Plan: [`bb-expansion-design.md`](../bb-expansion-design.md) §7a. Decisions
150–159. Branch `claude/bb-europe`.

The author's go after E1 was "Merge, make apk, and then go on". E2 is
content only, about 25,300 tokens across 23 notes: eleven new cities in the
shape of the approved Budapest sample, the author's six original cities
deepened in place (every fact and faction kept, each with a second faction,
places, people and a third hook), three wild places and three continent
notes (the Hollow Roads, Powers of the continent, Who's who), which reach
every Blood & Bone story on load through E1's rule.

Four writers worked in parallel from one brief. The read-through caught what
parallel writing produces: a faction name used twice (Kraków's Ledger House
and a new "Ledger Houses" in Vienna, now the Inheritors), three people named
Anselm, a surname used three times, three priests who hear confessions and
hunt, a real rail tunnel still in service written up as a sealed ruin, a
continent faction described as both never selling secrets and selling them,
and continent notes thinner than the cities they tie together (expanded).

No screenshots: nothing on screen changed beyond more notes in the Notes
page, which E1's devlog already shows. The reading copy is the artifact
linked from the PR.

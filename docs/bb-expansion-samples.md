# Blood & Bone expansion — E0 samples

Status: **for the author's yes on voice and depth** (decision 157, design
[`bb-expansion-design.md`](bb-expansion-design.md) §6). Nothing here ships
yet; once approved, these become part of E1 (the table family), E2 (the city)
and E3 (the Archetype). Original text throughout (decision 3).

Each sample is written exactly as the GM would read it in the app.

```
3 Waits at the door to be asked in. 4 Too still between movements.
5 A faint metal sweetness on their breath. 6 Mirrors in the room have been turned.

```

**Notes on the sample.** 2,070 tokens (cl100k), inside the 2,500 budget. The
names avoid famous people and the obvious horror-movie surnames. Entry 66 of
the look table and entry 20 of the secrets table are the "roll twice" outs,
kept to one per table. The tells are written to be noticed, not proved, so
they can't short-cut a mystery.

## Sample 2 — a city: Europe by Night — Budapest

```
# Europe by Night — Budapest

**Atmosphere:** Two cities that pretend to be one. Buda is hills, villas,
and a castle district that went quiet a long time ago and never told
anyone why. Pest is flat, loud, and young, its ruin bars built in the
courtyards of buildings nobody bothered to fix. Between them the Danube runs
fast and cold, and every bridge has been blown up at least once.

Underneath Buda, the hot springs. Thermal water has cut caves through the
hills for longer than there have been people to bathe in it, and the caves
connect to places that are not on any survey. The baths are where the night
does business. You cannot carry a weapon in a bathing suit, and nobody lies
easily in water that hot.

**Who holds it:** Witches, more than anywhere east of Paris, because the
springs carry power the way wires carry current. Vampires keep to Buda's
villas and the old money. Pest's nightlife belongs to nobody, which means
everybody is fighting over it.

**Faction — The Bathkeepers:** Five old families of Witches who between them
hold the rights to the springs, inherited, sold and stolen across three
centuries. They meet in the steam, they keep the peace in the water, and any
working that draws on the springs pays them a tithe. The Bathkeepers have
never agreed on anything except that the tithe is owed. One of the five
families died out last year; the other four have not yet said who inherits.

**Faction — The Lantern Club:** Pest's Vampires, younger, poorer and hungrier
than Buda's. They run the ruin bars, feed on the crowds and keep the
Concord badly. They think the Buda courts are museum pieces. The Buda courts
think the Lantern Club will be ash within a decade. Both are partly right.

**Local threat:** The springs are running hotter. Bathers have been scalded
in pools that held steady for two hundred years, and the deep caves are
filling with steam that carries voices. The Bathkeepers say it is the dead
family's wards failing. The truth is that someone is drawing on the springs
without paying, a great deal, and has been for months.

**Places:**
- **The Rudas night session.** Weekend nights the old Turkish bath opens
  late. Under the octagonal dome the Bathkeepers' neutral ground holds:
  no feeding, no workings, no violence in the water. What happens in the
  changing rooms is another matter.
- **The Fisherman's Bastion at 4 a.m.** Empty of tourists, white towers over
  the river, the city's best place for a meeting where both sides can see
  anyone coming.
- **A ruin bar called Kertész.** Seven courtyards, a different crowd in
  each, the Lantern Club's back office in the seventh. Nobody finds the
  seventh courtyard on their first visit.
- **The Hospital in the Rock.** A wartime hospital cut into Castle Hill,
  now a museum by day. The Ghosts of the siege still keep their wards, and
  they still triage whoever comes in.

**People:**
- **Margit Szalai**, eldest of the Bathkeepers, eighty, sharp, kind to
  children and to nobody else. She wants the dead family's rights for her
  granddaughter and will burn the Concord to get them.
- **Ábel Kovács**, the Lantern Club's founder, turned in 1989 and still
  dressed for it. Charming, broke, and the only Vampire in Pest the Buda
  courts will take a call from.
- **Dr. Réka Fodor**, a hydrogeologist measuring the springs for the city.
  Her readings don't make sense, and she has started going down into the
  caves alone to find out why.
- **Sister Anasztázia**, a Hunter who runs a soup kitchen near Keleti
  station. She feeds the hungry, all of them, and keeps a list of which ones
  aren't human.
- **The Siege Matron**, the Ghost who runs the Hospital in the Rock. She has
  never left and never will, and anyone wounded in Buda's tunnels ends up in
  her care whether they want it or not.

**Story hooks:**
- The dead family's heir turns up: a mortal student in Pest who has no idea
  what she has inherited, and four Witch families who each want her signed,
  sworn or gone.
- A Lantern Club Vampire is found boiled in a cave pool, and the Club blames
  the Bathkeepers. The Bathkeepers blame the Club. Someone wanted exactly
  that.
- Dr. Fodor's readings point to a chamber under Gellért Hill that is on no
  map. She has asked the characters to come down with her, because the last
  time she went alone something spoke to her in her mother's voice.

Use when the story is set in Budapest, or when the characters travel
there along the Danube.
```

**Notes on the sample.** 1,090 tokens, against about 350 for each
existing city: the depth the E2 cities would have. Real places (Rudas,
the Fisherman's Bastion, the Hospital in the Rock, Gellért Hill, Keleti) as
backdrop; the factions, people and the ruin bar Kertész are invented. No
real organisation is made supernatural. The local threat has a stated truth
so the GM can run it as a mystery.

## Sample 3 — an Archetype: the Vampire's Courtier

Written to slot under the Vampire's existing two Archetypes, in their exact
shape.

```
**The Courtier** — *Feeds only by arrangement: willing donors, standing
agreements, blood as a social currency.*
- Sate the Beast on a willing donor you have an arrangement with: on a
  strong hit, also choose one: the donor owes you a favour, or tells you
  something they heard. On a miss, the arrangement sours: the donor wants
  more from you than blood.
- Feeding by arrangement never risks The Mask Slips from the feeding
  itself. A donor who is hurt, frightened or cheated becomes a talker
  (Exposure +1 the first time they talk).
- +1 when you Persuade, Bargain or read someone across a table you set,
  in a room you chose.
- **Archetype XP:** 1 XP for feeding by arrangement through a whole night
  without once taking more than agreed; 3 XP for turning a donor's favour
  into leverage over someone who outranks you; 5 XP for building a standing
  arrangement with a household, a club or a faction that feeds you and
  keeps your secret.
- **Alternate XP:** 1 XP for hosting a gathering where the Kinds meet and
  nobody bleeds; 3 XP for keeping a donor safe when keeping them cost you;
  5 XP for being invited, openly and by name, into a mortal home you will
  never be asked to leave.
- **Stress Relief:** an evening of good company where you are wanted for
  more than what you are; a donor who comes back because they choose to;
  setting a table and watching other people eat.
```

**Notes on the sample.** 340 tokens. It keeps the Kind's rules intact (sunlight,
thresholds) and bends Sate the Beast and the Masquerade, as the other two
Archetypes do. Its fiction is the one neither existing Archetype covers:
the Abstinent refuses and the Ripper indulges; the Courtier negotiates.
The "talker" rule reuses the core's Exposure trigger rather than inventing
a new one.
